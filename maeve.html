bio01/09/2026

six-piece from south wales making loud, wordy, gritty post-punk with shoegaze hooks and haze. raw and unapologetic, trading polish for noise, sweat and unending emotion.

together only since march 2026, they've introduced themselves at weekend rumble, shunkfest and orange light festival. supported dead dads club, about to share the bbc introducing live bill with the bug club, booked for sŵn festival and sold out venues in newport and cardiff, with bigger dates to come in 2027…

debut single ‘behind your smile’, produced by skindred founding guitarist jeff rose, earned bbc introducing wales airplay and a native radio discovery of the week nod. next up is ‘sacrifice’, recorded with rockfield producer nick brine (teenage fanclub, nada surf).
